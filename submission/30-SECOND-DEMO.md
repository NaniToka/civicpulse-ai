## ⚡ 30-Second Rapid Runbook

| Seconds | Action & Screen | Presenter Script |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |

| **16–24s** | Open `/recommendations`, click `View Evidence Trail`. | *"Every priority recommendation is backed by a 6-step Evidence Trail—'Show Your Work'—linking citizen voices to capacity gap metrics."* |
| **24–30s** | Open `/scenarios`, click `Execute Counterfactual Simulation`. | *"Our Scenario Lab lets policymakers simulate budget allocations before spending public funds. Feedback becomes decision intelligence."* |
