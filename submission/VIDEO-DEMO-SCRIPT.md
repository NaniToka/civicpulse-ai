## 📽️ Screen Interaction & Narration Script

### Scene 1: Executive Overview (00:00 – 00:10)
- **ACTION**: Open browser to `http://localhost:5173`. Position mouse over hero banner.
- **SCREEN**: Executive Overview landing view showing hero banner and top KPI cards.
- **SAY**: *"Every year, governments process millions of citizen complaints in isolated silos. CivicPulse AI builds the first real Civic Decision Intelligence layer for Digital Public Infrastructure—transforming multilingual citizen voices into evidence-backed capital investment priorities."*

---

### Scene 2: Executive Dashboard (00:10 – 00:25)
- **ACTION**: Hover over the **Civic Demand Landscape** heatmap cards and scroll smoothly to the **Live Signal Ticker**.
- **SCREEN**: Regional heatmap cards (Kanpur South, Ekurhuleni East, Pune Ward 12) with per-capita demand indicators.
- **SAY**: *"Instead of counting raw complaints, CivicPulse normalizes demand per 100,000 residents against census vulnerability indices and operational capacity deficits."*

---

### Scene 3: Multilingual Telugu Voice Input (00:25 – 00:50)
- **ACTION**: Click `Demand Intelligence` (`/demand`) on the sidebar. Click the Telugu sample button: `Telugu: Healthcare Access` (`మా ప్రాంతంలో పిల్లలకు మంచి ఆసుపత్రి లేదు.`).
- **SCREEN**: `CitizenVoiceComposer` populates with Telugu script text.
- **SAY**: *"Let's test a real citizen signal in Telugu: 'మా ప్రాంతంలో పిల్లలకు మంచి ఆసుపత్రి లేదు.'—stating that there is no good pediatric hospital in the locality."*

---

### Scene 4: AI Signal Extraction (00:50 – 01:15)
- **ACTION**: Click `Analyze Civic Signal`.
- **SCREEN**: `Extracted Civic Intelligence Signal` card animates in, showing:
  - Language: `TE` (Telugu, 98% confidence)
  - Category: `Healthcare`
  - Urgency: `HIGH`
  - Normalized English Meaning: `"Lacks adequate hospital and pediatric healthcare facilities in the locality."`
- **SAY**: *"Our NLP engine detects script `TE`, classifies intent as `Healthcare Improvement`, assigns `HIGH` urgency, and normalizes the meaning into English. Adding this signal updates live regional demand concentration."*
- **ACTION**: Click `Add Signal to Civic Intelligence`.

---

### Scene 5: Traceable 6-Step Evidence Trail (01:15 – 01:35)
- **ACTION**: Click `Recommendations` (`/recommendations`) on the sidebar. Click `View Evidence Trail` on Kanpur South Healthcare Expansion (#1 Priority, Score `91.4/100`).
- **SCREEN**: `EvidenceTrailModal` opens displaying the vertical 6-step evidence chain:
  `01 DEMAND VOICES → 02 VELOCITY → 03 GAP SCORE → 04 DEMOGRAPHIC NEED → 05 INVESTMENT OVERLAP → 06 RECOMMENDATION`
- **SAY**: *"Every priority is backed by a 6-step Evidence Trail—'Show Your Work'—allowing policymakers to trace recommendations back to verified citizen demand and capacity deficit metrics."*
- **ACTION**: Click `TE` in the language switcher to show Telugu decision brief. Click `ESC` to close modal.

---

### Scene 6: Scenario Lab Budget Simulation (01:35 – 01:50)
- **ACTION**: Click `Scenario Lab` (`/scenarios`) on the sidebar. Select `Kanpur South Belt`, `Healthcare`, set budget to `$15,000,000 USD`. Click `Execute Counterfactual Simulation`.
- **SCREEN**: Simulation results card renders with priority score delta (`-18.5 pts`) and projected beneficiaries (`~730,000 residents`).
- **SAY**: *"In our Scenario Lab, policymakers can test counterfactual budget interventions—seeing a $15M USD allocation reduce healthcare deficit intensity and benefit ~730,000 residents before public capital is spent."*

---

### Scene 7: Closing Statement (01:50 – 02:00)
- **ACTION**: Return to landing dashboard (`/`).
- **SCREEN**: Clean Executive Overview cockpit view.
- **SAY**: *"Most civic platforms stop at collecting complaints. CivicPulse turns citizen voice into evidence, evidence into priorities, and priorities into testable decisions. Citizen voice is not just collected—it becomes decision intelligence."*
