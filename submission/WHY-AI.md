# CivicPulse AI — Why Use AI?

---

## 🧠 The Necessary Role of AI in Civic Intelligence

Citizens do not submit standardized database records or fill out complex government taxonomy forms. They express urgent community needs naturally in their native languages:

- **Telugu**: *"మా ప్రాంతంలో పిల్లలకు మంచి ఆసుపత్రి లేదు."*
- **Hindi**: *"हमारे इलाके में पीने के पानी की समस्या है।"*
- **Marathi**: *"आमच्या भागात पिण्याच्या पाण्याची पाइपलाइन फुटली आहे।"*
- **Portuguese**: *"Sem esgoto encanado nem tratamento de água no bairro."*
- **Zulu**: *"Asinamanzi ahlelekile kule ndawo."*

---

## 🔑 What AI Does in CivicPulse
1. **Script-Aware Language Detection**: Identifies Unicode script ranges (Telugu `\u0C00-\u0C7F`, Devanagari `\u0900-\u097F`, Bengali, Latin).
2. **Translation & Meaning Normalization**: Translates raw local feedback into normalized English representations for cross-district comparison.
3. **Civic Intent Classification**: Maps unstructured text into 10 controlled intents (`request_improvement`, `report_outage`, `report_service_gap`, etc.).
4. **Entity & Urgency Parsing**: Extracts facility types, target beneficiary groups, and urgency levels.
5. **Multilingual Policymaker Briefs**: Generates executive decision summaries in English, Hindi, or Telugu.

---

## 🛑 What AI Does NOT Do
- AI **never** computes priority scores ($0.0 \text{ to } 100.0$).
- AI **never** alters census demographic metrics or population statistics.
- AI **never** modifies infrastructure capacity gap calculations.

AI removes language barriers; deterministic Python code computes decisions.
