# Novel Combination of Capabilities & Innovation Claim

## Evidence-Based Innovation Breakdown
Rather than relying on unverified superlatives ("first in the world", "revolutionary"), this document articulates the specific, verifiable combination of technological capabilities that constitutes the core innovation of CivicPulse AI.

---

## The 5 Combined Capabilities

1. **Multilingual Native Script Signal Extraction**:
   Integrates script-aware language identification (Telugu, Devanagari, Bengali, Latin) and structured entity extraction to capture raw citizen voice signals across low-resource language environments.

2. **Per-Capita Demand Density Normalization**:
   Normalizes complaint density against regional census populations ($100\text{k}$ resident baseline), preventing high-density metropolitan wards from overwhelming lower-density rural districts.

3. **Hybrid AI / Deterministic Boundary Architecture**:
   Employs Google Gemini strictly for language understanding, intent extraction, and multilingual translation, while enforcing a strict boundary where all priority scoring, deficit calculations, and scenario simulations are performed by 100% deterministic Python code.

4. **Machine-Readable 6-Step Evidence Trail ("Show Your Work")**:
   Generates a fully traceable visual and machine-readable evidence chain (`01 Demand → 02 Momentum → 03 Gap → 04 Demographic → 05 Investment → 06 Priority`), allowing decision-makers and transparency auditors to verify every factor contribution.

5. **Counterfactual Capital Allocation Scenario Lab**:
   Provides an interactive policy simulator that evaluates hypothetical budget allocations ($\$1\text{M}$ to $\$50\text{M USD}$) against infrastructure capacity curves to compute score deltas and projected beneficiaries prior to financial commitment.
