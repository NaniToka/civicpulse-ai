# CivicPulse AI — Social Impact Story & Vision

---

## 🌍 The Social Impact Opportunity

In many developing economies and BRICS municipalities, public infrastructure investments are made based on legacy political allocations rather than real citizen demand. Non-English-speaking citizens in peri-urban wards and rural districts are frequently excluded from municipal priority setting.

**CivicPulse AI transforms this equation:**
- **Inclusion**: Gives native speakers of Telugu, Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Zulu, and Portuguese direct voice representation.
- **Equity**: Per-capita normalization ($100k$ baseline) ensures small rural populations receive equal per-resident weight against large metropolitan hubs.
- **Transparency**: 6-step evidence trails allow civil society organizations to inspect why public capital was allocated.
