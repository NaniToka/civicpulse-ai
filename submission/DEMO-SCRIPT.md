## ⏱️ 90-Second Presentation Runbook

- **0–10s (Executive Overview)**: Show landing dashboard banner. Highlight mission statement: *"Transforming multilingual citizen voices into evidence-driven capital investment priorities."*
- **10–25s (Multilingual Signal Ingestion)**: Navigate to `Demand Intelligence` tab (`/demand`). Click Telugu quick sample button: *"మా ప్రాంతంలో పిల్లలకు మంచి ఆసుపత్రి లేదు."* Click `Analyze Civic Signal`. Show script detection (`TE`), category (`HEALTHCARE`), intent (`REQUEST_IMPROVEMENT`), urgency (`HIGH`), confidence (`94%`), and normalized English meaning. Click `Add Signal to Civic Intelligence`.
- **25–40s (Priority Recommendations)**: Navigate to `Recommendations` tab (`/recommendations`). Highlight Kanpur South Healthcare Expansion (#1 Priority, Score: `91.4/100`).
- **40–55s (Evidence Trail Inspection)**: Click `View Evidence Trail`. Walk through the 6-step evidence chain:
  `01 CITIZEN DEMAND → 02 DEMAND MOMENTUM → 03 INFRASTRUCTURE GAP → 04 DEMOGRAPHIC NEED → 05 INVESTMENT OVERLAP → 06 PRIORITY RECOMMENDATION`
  Toggle language switcher (**EN / HI / TE**) to display Telugu AI decision brief.
- **55–70s (Scenario Lab)**: Navigate to `Scenario Lab` tab (`/scenarios`). Select `Kanpur South`, `Healthcare`, `$15,000,000 USD` budget, `+20%` coverage addition. Click `Execute Counterfactual Simulation`. Show priority score delta (`-18.5 pts`) and projected citizen beneficiaries (`~730,000 residents`).
- **70–90s (Closing Statement)**: Summarize Responsible AI boundary: Google Gemini processes natural language; deterministic Python logic computes all scores and gap metrics.
