# CivicPulse AI — Presenter Slide-by-Slide Rehearsal Script

---

## 🗣️ Slide Narration Runbook

### Slide 1: Title & Value Proposition
- **SCREEN**: Slide 1 (CivicPulse AI Title Header)
- **SAY**: *"Good morning. I'm presenting CivicPulse AI—an open-source civic decision intelligence platform that transforms multilingual citizen voices into traceable, evidence-backed civic investment priorities."*
- **CLICK**: Advance to Slide 2.

---

### Slide 2: The Problem
- **SCREEN**: Slide 2 (The Civic Governance Challenge)
- **SAY**: *"Governments across developing nations process millions of citizen complaints every year. But traditional portals process feedback in silos. Unstructured voices across Telugu, Hindi, or Zulu are lost, while public capital is allocated without real-time, per-capita demand visibility."*
- **CLICK**: Advance to Slide 3.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution
- **SCREEN**: Slide 3 (Civic Decision Intelligence Engine)
- **SAY**: *"CivicPulse bridges citizen feedback and national public works planning. We convert raw multilingual text into structured civic signals, connect them with census vulnerability indices and operational capacity gap scores, and output ranked priority recommendations."*
- **CLICK**: Advance to Slide 4.

---

### Slide 4: Responsible AI Architecture
- **SCREEN**: Slide 4 (Responsible AI Boundary Architecture)
- **SAY**: *"Crucially, we enforce a strict architectural boundary: Google Gemini handles natural language understanding, script detection, and translation. Deterministic Python code computes all priority scores, gap indices, and scenario deltas. AI understands language; Python computes the decision."*
- **CLICK**: Advance to Slide 5.

---

### Slide 5: The 6-Step Evidence Trail
- **SCREEN**: Slide 5 (Show Your Work Evidence Trail)
- **SAY**: *"Every priority recommendation features a 6-step Evidence Trail—'Show Your Work'—linking raw citizen demand signals directly to capacity deficit calculations and capital project overlap penalties."*
- **CLICK**: Advance to Slide 6.

---

### Slide 6: Counterfactual Scenario Lab
- **SCREEN**: Slide 6 (Scenario Lab Budget Simulator)
- **SAY**: *"In our Scenario Lab, policymakers can simulate hypothetical budget interventions—seeing a $15M USD allocation in Kanpur South reduce healthcare capacity deficits and benefit ~730,000 residents before public funds are spent."*
- **CLICK**: Advance to Slide 7.

---

### Slide 7: Security & Governance
- **SCREEN**: Slide 7 (Security Controls & Responsible AI)
- **SAY**: *"Our platform includes prompt injection defense, 10MB payload size limits, rate limiting, and rule-based fallback resilience to ensure zero downtime."*
- **CLICK**: Advance to Slide 8.

---

### Slide 8: Future & Closing
- **SCREEN**: Slide 8 (Future Roadmap & Final Closing)
- **SAY**: *"Most civic platforms stop at collecting complaints. CivicPulse goes one step further. It turns citizen voice into evidence, evidence into priorities, and priorities into testable decisions. Citizen voice is not just collected—it becomes decision intelligence."*
