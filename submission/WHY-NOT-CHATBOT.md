# CivicPulse AI — Why Not Just a Chatbot?

---

## 🤖 Chatbot vs. Decision Intelligence Platform

A chatbot produces text responses to individual prompts. **CivicPulse AI is an enterprise decision intelligence layer** for public infrastructure planning.

| Dimension | Generic LLM Chatbot | CivicPulse AI Platform |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Output Type** | Conversational text output | Traceable 6-step evidence trail & priority score ($0.0\text{--}100.0$) |
| **Data Normalization**| None (processes prompt in isolation) | Normalizes demand per 100,000 residents vs census vulnerability |
| **Decision Logic** | Non-deterministic LLM hallucination risk | Deterministic 8-factor Python mathematical formula (Engine V2) |
| **Public Auditability**| Zero audit trail | Transparent factor contributions & active capital project checks |
| **Policy Tools** | Text summaries | Counterfactual Scenario Lab simulator ($1M to $50M USD budget deltas) |
| **Governance Role** | Conversational agent | Decision-support platform for public works commissions |

```
       ↓ (Counterfactual Simulator)
Scenario Lab Budget Intervention Delta
```
