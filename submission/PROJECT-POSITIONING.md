# CivicPulse AI — Final Project Positioning

**Product**: CivicPulse AI  
**Track**: Track 1 — AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance (BRICS Nations)  

---

## 📌 One-Line Description
> *"An open-source civic decision intelligence layer that transforms multilingual citizen voices into traceable, evidence-backed civic investment priorities."*

---

## 🏛️ Problem Statement
Across BRICS nations and developing economies, public administrations process millions of citizen complaints every year. Traditional complaint portals collect feedback in isolated silos, leaving unstructured citizen voices across multiple native languages (Hindi, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Zulu, Portuguese, English) untranslated, unaggregated, and disconnected from public investment planning. As a result, governments spend billions on infrastructure without real-time, per-capita demand visibility.

---

## 💡 Solution
CivicPulse AI converts unstructured multilingual citizen requests into structured civic signals, connects them with geographic demand concentration, operational infrastructure capacity gaps, census demographic vulnerability indices, and active capital project overlap analysis, and outputs transparent, ranked priority recommendations for policymakers.

---

## 🔑 Core Differentiator
The platform does **NOT** ask an LLM: *"What should the government build?"*

Instead, CivicPulse AI enforces a strict architectural boundary:
- **Google Gemini AI** understands natural language (script detection, translation, intent classification, entity parsing, multilingual decision briefs).
- **Deterministic Python Engine V2** computes priority scores ($0.0 \text{ to } 100.0$), per-capita population demand normalization ($100k$ baseline), capacity deficit indices, and counterfactual scenario deltas.
- **Traceable 6-Step Evidence Trail** ("Show Your Work") proves exactly why a project was recommended without forcing policymakers to trust an opaque LLM black box.
- **Scenario Simulator** allows policymakers to test budget allocations ($1M to $50M USD) before committing public capital.
