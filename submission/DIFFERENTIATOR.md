# CivicPulse AI — 8 Core Differentiators Summary

---

## 🏛️ The 8 Pillars of CivicPulse AI

1. **Multilingual Voice Understanding**: Script-aware language detection across Telugu, Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Portuguese, Zulu, and English text inputs.
2. **Per-Capita Demand Normalization**: Demand density normalized per 100,000 residents to ensure equal voice representation.
3. **Infrastructure Capacity Gap Analysis**: Operational deficit indexing across healthcare, water, electricity, transit, digital connectivity, and sanitation.
4. **Demographic Vulnerability Context**: Integration of census demographic vulnerability indices.
5. **Capital Investment Overlap Analysis**: Automatic detection of active duplicate projects (-15.0 pt penalty) or delayed projects (+10.0 pt boost).
6. **Deterministic Priority Engine V2**: 8-factor mathematical scoring model ($0.0 \text{ to } 100.0$).
7. **Traceable 6-Step Evidence Trail**: Visual timeline ("Show Your Work") linking citizen voices to capital recommendations.
8. **Counterfactual Scenario Lab**: Simulator allowing policymakers to test budget allocations ($1M to $50M USD) with score deltas and beneficiary projections.
