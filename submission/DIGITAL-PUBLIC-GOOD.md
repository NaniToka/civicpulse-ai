# CivicPulse AI — Open-Source Digital Public Good Orientation

---

## 🌐 Open-Source & DPG Design Principles

CivicPulse AI is engineered as an **open-source, digital-public-good-oriented prototype**:

1. **Permissive Open Licensing**: Published under the open-source **MIT License** (`LICENSE`), permitting unrestricted reuse, deployment, and adaptation by public administrations.
2. **Transparent Responsible AI**: Explicit separation between LLM language parsing and deterministic mathematical scoring (`docs/responsible-ai.md`).
3. **No Vendor Lock-In**: Supports both Google Gemini and offline zero-dependency `RuleBasedLanguageIntelligenceProvider`.
4. **Reproducible Deployment**: Fully containerized with `Dockerfile` and `docker-compose.yml`.
5. **Open Governance**: Complete open-source documentation suite (`README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`).
