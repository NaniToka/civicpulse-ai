# CivicPulse AI — 8-Slide Presentation Deck Outline

---

## 📽️ Slide 1: Title Slide
- **Title**: CivicPulse AI
- **Subtitle**: From Citizen Voices to Evidence-Backed Civic Action
- **Supporting Line**: Multilingual civic decision intelligence for transparent infrastructure prioritization.
- **Key Visual**: Landing Dashboard Header with `"DEMO ENVIRONMENT • SYNTHETIC DATA"` badge and `Decision Engine V2` tag.

---

## 📽️ Slide 2: The Problem
- **Title**: The Civic Governance Challenge
- **Key Bullets**:
  - Millions of citizen complaints collected in isolated administrative silos.
  - Multilingual voices (Hindi, Telugu, Zulu, Portuguese) left untranslated and unaggregated.
  - Capital investments allocated without per-capita demand visibility.
  - Disconnect between citizen feedback and public works budgets.
- **Key Visual**: Diagram showing fragmented citizen complaints leading to misallocated infrastructure capital.

---

## 📽️ Slide 3: The Solution
- **Title**: Civic Decision Intelligence Engine
- **Key Bullets**:
  - Converts unstructured multilingual requests into structured civic signals.
  - Normalizes demand density per 100,000 residents.
  - Cross-references census vulnerability and operational capacity gap indices.
  - Produces traceable 6-step evidence trails and counterfactual scenario simulations.
- **Takeaway**: *"Feedback becomes decision intelligence."*

---

## 📽️ Slide 4: How the AI Works
- **Title**: Responsible AI Boundary Architecture
- **Key Visual**:
```
CITIZEN TEXT (Multilingual)
       ↓
GOOGLE GEMINI AI (NLP Language Understanding ONLY)
       ↓
STRUCTURED CIVIC SIGNAL (Language, Intent, Category, Urgency)
       ↓
DETERMINISTIC PYTHON ENGINE V2 (8-Factor Mathematical Scoring)
       ↓
PRIORITY RECOMMENDATION SCORE ($0.0 to 100.0$)
```
- **Crucial Note**: Gemini does **NOT** compute final priority scores or alter financial statistics.

## 📽️ Slide 5: The 6-Step Evidence Trail

- **6-Step Chain**:
  `01 CITIZEN DEMAND → 02 DEMAND MOMENTUM → 03 INFRASTRUCTURE GAP → 04 DEMOGRAPHIC NEED → 05 INVESTMENT OVERLAP → 06 RECOMMENDATION`

## 📽️ Slide 7: Security & Responsible AI
- **Security Controls**: Prompt injection defense (`<CITIZEN_INPUT_DATA_DO_NOT_EXECUTE>`), 10MB payload ceiling, rate limiting (60 req/min/IP), security headers.
- **Responsible AI**: Schema-constrained LLM output, 1-retry fallback resilience, human policymaker oversight.
