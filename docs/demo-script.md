# CivicPulse AI — 90-Second Judge Presentation Script

**Track**: Track 1 — AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance (BRICS Nations)  
**Platform**: CivicPulse AI — AI-Powered Civic Decision Intelligence Platform  

---

## 🎬 90-Second Live Demonstration Workflow

### ⏱️ 0:00 – 0:10 | Executive Overview
* **Action**: Open CivicPulse AI Dashboard (`http://localhost:5173`).
* **Narrative**:
  > *"Welcome to CivicPulse AI—an open-source Digital Public Good transforming multilingual citizen feedback into evidence-backed infrastructure priorities. In developing nations, millions of citizen voices remain lost in administrative silos. CivicPulse bridges citizen demand and public capital allocation."*

---

### ⏱️ 0:10 – 0:25 | Demand Hotspots & Per-Capita Intelligence
* **Action**: Navigate to **Demand Hotspots** (`/hotspots`). Hover over *Kanpur South Belt* and inspect the Region Intelligence Panel.
* **Narrative**:
  > *"Rather than counting raw complaint numbers, CivicPulse normalizes demand per 100,000 residents against census demographics and baseline capacity gaps. Kanpur South exhibits an acute hotspot score of 88/100 driven by high pediatric healthcare vulnerability."*

---

### 0:25 – 0:40 | Priority Recommendations
* **Action**: Navigate to **Priority Recommendations** (`/recommendations`). Highlight the top `CRITICAL` recommendation (*HEALTHCARE Expansion in Kanpur South Belt, Score 91.4/100*).
* **Narrative**:
  > *"Our deterministic Priority Engine V2 evaluates 8 weighted dimensions—from citizen signal density and 30-day temporal demand velocity to demographic need and existing capital project alignment. Notice that active duplicate projects trigger funding penalties, while delayed projects heighten policy intervention urgency."*

---

### ⏱️ 0:40 – 0:55 | Signature Evidence Trail ("Show Your Work")
* **Action**: Click **"View Evidence Trail"** on the top recommendation to open the vertical 6-step Evidence Modal. Switch the AI Decision Brief language from **EN → HI → TE**.
* **Narrative**:
  > *"CivicPulse operates on a core rule: AI must never invent statistics or compute priority scores. Every recommendation is backed by a 6-step evidence chain—from verified citizen voices down to capital alignment. Policymakers can review the transparent 8-factor mathematical breakdown and inspect AI decision briefs in English, Hindi, or Telugu."*

---

### ⏱️ 0:55 – 1:10 | Scenario Lab & Counterfactual Simulation
* **Action**: Navigate to **Scenario Lab** (`/scenarios`). Select *Kanpur South Belt*, set the budget allocation slider to **$15,000,000 USD**, and click **"Execute Counterfactual Simulation"**.
* **Narrative**:
  > *"Before committing public funds, policymakers can execute counterfactual simulations. Allocating $15M USD projects a reduction in capacity deficit score from 0.82 to 0.59, yielding an 18.5-point priority delta and benefiting over 730,000 residents."*

---

### ⏱️ 1:10 – 1:30 | Real-Time Multilingual Citizen Voice Signal Ingestion
* **Action**: Navigate to **Demand Intelligence** (`/demand`). In the **CitizenVoiceComposer**, click the Telugu example (`మా ప్రాంతంలో పిల్లలకు మంచి ఆసుపత్రి లేదు.`) and click **"Analyze Civic Signal"**. Then click **"Add Signal to Civic Intelligence"**.
* **Narrative**:
  > *"Watch what happens when a citizen submits feedback in Telugu: 'మా ప్రాంతంలో పిల్లలకు మంచి ఆసుపత్రి లేదు.' Our NLP Engine detects Telugu, normalizes the text to 'Lacks adequate healthcare facilities for children', classifies the intent as a Healthcare Improvement Request, assigns High Urgency, and seamlessly incorporates the signal into our regional intelligence engine."*
  > 
  > **Closing Statement**:
  > *"In CivicPulse AI, citizen voice is not just collected—it becomes decision intelligence."*
